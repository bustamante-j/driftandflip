# Your cross → the 3D cross in the hero

Drop **one image of the cross** here (PNG, JPG or WebP). The site turns it into the
spinning 3D chrome cross behind the logo.

What works best:

- **Straight-on, front view.** Not tilted or at an angle.
- **The cross only**, on a transparent or plain background (black or white).
- **Big**: about 1000 px tall or more.
- Engravings and texture on the cross show up as raised detail on the front.

How it works: the outline is traced into a 3D shape with chrome bevelled edges, and the
image's detail is pressed onto the front face. If more than one image is here, the newest
one is used.

The new cross goes live when the site is next deployed. Until then, a built-in gothic
cross is shown.
